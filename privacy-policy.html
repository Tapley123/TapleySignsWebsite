Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Tapley Signs (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Irish data protection law.

2. What Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you contact us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The content of any message or enquiry you send us

We do not collect any special category data (such as health or financial information).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Communicate with you about a project we are working on together

We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the basis of legitimate interests — specifically, to respond to business enquiries you have initiated — and, where applicable, to fulfil a contract with you.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain contact and project correspondence for up to 3 years after the last interaction, after which it is securely deleted. Financial records related to invoicing are retained for 7 years in line with Irish Revenue requirements.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at infotapleys@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics software. The only third-party content loaded is a Google Maps embed on our contact page, which may set cookies in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact & Complaints

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at infotapleys@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.
