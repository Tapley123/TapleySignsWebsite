About Us

We are a family-run business in operation since 2004 with considerable experience in the manufacturing and installation of an extensive range of high-quality graphics. The core of our business is in-store graphics — window, floor, counter, rail, escalator and till graphics — but we also produce large and small-scale banners, lightboxes, vinyl wraps, self-adhesive stickers, construction site signage and much more.

Using the latest in digital print technology, our specialist team of printers and fitters will ensure that your concepts and designs create and deliver the expected visual impact — on time and to the highest standard.

We handle multi-location rollouts across the whole of Ireland — coordinating simultaneous installations across Dublin, Cork, Limerick and Galway. Consistent quality, delivered on schedule, nationwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install nationwide?

Yes — we install across the whole of Ireland. We regularly manage multi-branch rollouts covering Dublin, Cork, Limerick and Galway in a single coordinated project.

What types of surfaces can you apply graphics to?

Almost any surface — glass, walls, floors, ceilings, vehicles, scaffolding, hoardings, retail fixtures and more. If you have an unusual application in mind, get in touch and we’ll work out the right solution.

Do you offer a design service?

We work with print-ready artwork supplied by you or your agency. If your artwork isn’t quite ready for print, we can advise on adjustments. For new design work, we can recommend trusted design partners.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept print-ready PDF, AI or EPS files. Images should be at least 300dpi at the intended print size. High-resolution TIFF or JPEG files are also accepted. If you’re unsure, send us what you have and we’ll advise.

What are your typical turnaround times?

Turnaround depends on the size and complexity of the job. Standard print and installation work is typically completed within 5–10 working days from approval of artwork. For large-scale or multi-location projects, we’ll agree a detailed schedule with you upfront.
