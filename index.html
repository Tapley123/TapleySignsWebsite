About Us

We are a family-run business in operation since 2004 with considerable experience in the manufacturing and installation of an extensive range of high-quality graphics. The core of our business is in-store graphics — window, floor, counter, rail, escalator and till graphics — but we also produce large and small-scale banners, lightboxes, vinyl wraps, self-adhesive stickers, construction site signage and much more.

Using the latest in digital print technology, our specialist team of printers and fitters will ensure that your concepts and designs create and deliver the expected visual impact — on time and to the highest standard.

Services

What we offer

In-Store Graphics

Window, floor, counter, rail, escalator and till graphics that transform retail interiors into immersive brand experiences. Trusted by Primark, Brown Thomas and leading retailers across Ireland.

Vinyl & Mesh Banners

Large and small-scale vinyl and mesh banners for outdoor advertising, events, and construction hoardings. Durable, weather-resistant, and produced to exacting colour standards.

Building Wraps

High-impact large-format building wraps that command attention. From scaffolding graphics to full facade treatments, we handle projects of all scales for clients including Intel and Aviva.


Lightboxes

Illuminated lightbox signage for retail, hospitality and commercial environments. Maximum brand visibility in any lighting condition, built to last indoors and out.

Stickers & Vinyl

Custom self-adhesive vinyl stickers, decals and cut vinyl graphics for every application — from vehicle livery and window manifestations to branded packaging and product labelling.

Professional Installation

Our specialist team of fitters ensures every job is installed to the highest standard, on schedule and on budget. We cover sites across Dublin and nationwide.
